Augmento is a desktop app for data scientists, researchers and developers. Augment, rename and resize whole folders of images in a few clicks.

Download Augmento See what's new in version 2

What Augmento does

Computer vision models learn better from varied data. Augmento creates that variety from the images you already have, and gets your folders ready for training. See examples.

  • 25 augmentations, including rotation, flipping, perspective warp, noise, blur and color shifts.
  • Works on whole folders at once and saves the results where you choose.
  • Shows you how many images your dataset will contain before you generate.

What's new in version 2

Version 2 goes beyond single-image effects. It keeps your annotations in sync, gives you control over the results, and adds the dataset clean-up tools you would otherwise run as separate scripts.

New tools

  • Rename. Number every image in a folder (1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg and so on) so you always know how many you have. Label files and subfolders are handled, and existing files are never overwritten.
  • Resize. Set any size, such as 640x640 or 720x720, and choose to stretch, pad (letterbox) or center crop.

Better augmentation

  • Bounding box labels. YOLO .txt files are transformed together with the images, so flips, rotations and crops keep your boxes in the right place.
  • Combined pipelines. Mix several augmentations on one image, each with its own chance of being applied.
  • Multiple copies. Generate as many variations of each image as you need.
  • Intensity, seed and border fill. Control how strong the effects are, repeat a run exactly, and choose how empty corners are filled.
  • Live preview. Check a random sample before you generate thousands of images.

Faster and safer

  • Background processing. A progress bar and a Cancel button keep the window responsive on large datasets.
  • Your folders, your formats. Subfolders and file types are preserved, and a corrupt file is skipped and logged instead of stopping the run.
  • More reliable images. Grayscale, GIF and transparent files work, and phone photos load the right way up.
  • Fixes. Channel dropping, noise, perspective warp, hue shift and motion blur now behave correctly.
