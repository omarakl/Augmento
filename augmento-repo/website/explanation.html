Dataset tools in version 2

Two extra tabs prepare your folders before you augment them.

Rename

Renames every image in a folder to 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg and so on, so the last number is your image count.

Numbers follow natural order (2 before 10) and can be zero padded (001.jpg). Matching label files are renamed with their images, nothing is overwritten, and an optional rename_map.csv records the old names.

Resize

Resizes a whole folder to one size, such as 640x640 or 720x720, and saves the results in a new folder. Your originals are never changed.
